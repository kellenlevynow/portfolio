Research & IP

The question: can vineyards use less water under hot, dry conditions without giving up yield or grape quality? Grapes make an ideal model. Their physiology and form visibly register environmental stress, and thousands of years of cultivation knowledge provide a deep baseline to measure against.

  1. Common garden35 varieties grown side by side in Shasta County, CA
  2. TreatmentsIrrigation levels and a simulated drought
  3. Proximal sensingOptical and thermal sampling at leaf and canopy scale
  4. AssaysPhysiology, structure, yield and berry chemistry
  5. SelectionCultivars resilient to heat and drought

Conceptual model of the study hypothesis

Hypotheses

Balance

Resilience shows up as an optimal response, read from combined optical and thermal metrics, between too little light for growth and too much under stress.

Recovery

Resilient vines recover more effectively from heat and drought and hold on to more of their growth and productivity.

Quality

Vines that show the optimal response also carry superior berry chemistry for winemaking.

Method

Demonstrated methods for establishing drought-tolerant vines suited to dry farming or limited irrigation.

Dry-farming ready

Selection

Protocols for identifying cultivars that tolerate reduced irrigation and stay resilient under heat and drought.

Cultivar protocols

Reach

Lower water use while maintaining yield and quality, with methods that extend to other crops facing hot, dry conditions.

Methodology

The problem: lexical retrieval treats every occurrence of "micropiles" as equally probative. In practice the term appears in keyword-stuffed landscaping solicitations, in explicit scope exclusions, and in foundation designs where micropiles are merely one alternative under consideration. Every false positive demanded a full manual read, so analyst workload scaled with noise rather than with genuine opportunity.

  1. IngestProgrammatic acquisition of open solicitations
  2. PreprocessNormalize and segment scope, specs and bid schedules
  3. AnalyzeClaude performs document-level contextual reasoning
  4. ClassifyTernary verdict with cited evidentiary support

Decision Framework

Contextual Semantics

Provenance of the term, not its presence

A mention carries evidentiary weight only in proportion to where it occurs. Scope narratives, bid schedules and technical specifications are probative; keyword lists, citations and exclusions are not.

  • Scope and bid schedules weighted first
  • Exclusions and alternates detected
  • Keyword stuffing discounted

Evidentiary Grounding

Every determination is auditable

Each verdict is accompanied by the specific passages that support or undermine it, so reviewers can audit the reasoning without re-reading the solicitation.

  • Cited supporting passages
  • Countervailing signals surfaced
  • Calibrated confidence per verdict

Human-in-the-Loop Governance

Abstention under uncertainty

Where the record is ambiguous or the evidence thin, the agent abstains rather than guesses, routing the solicitation to an analyst who retains final authority.

  • Dedicated adjudication queue
  • Confirm or override in one action
  • Analysts retain final authority
