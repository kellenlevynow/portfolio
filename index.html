Decision Logic

Context Over Keywords

Reads the contract, not the word list

Where the term appears matters. Scope of work, bid items and specifications count; keyword lists, references and exclusions do not.

  • Scope and bid items weighed first
  • Exclusions and alternates detected
  • Keyword stuffing ignored

Evidence-Based Verdicts

Every call shows its reasons

Each verdict comes with the passages that support it, so a reviewer can check the reasoning in seconds instead of rereading the contract.

  • Quoted supporting passages
  • Signals for and against
  • Confidence on every verdict

Human in the Loop

Uncertain calls go to a person

When the contract is ambiguous or the evidence is thin, the agent does not guess. It routes the opportunity to an analyst to confirm or override.

  • Dedicated review queue
  • Confirm or override in one click
  • Analysts keep the final say
